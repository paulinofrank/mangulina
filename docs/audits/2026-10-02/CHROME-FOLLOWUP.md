## New official social evidence, still requiring a recording match

[Aramis Camilo's official Facebook video](https://www.facebook.com/aramiscamilooficial/videos/664385919336095/), posted March 29, 2025, states that he made a merengue adaptation of Hello with Mundo Records in 1984. This confirms his recording history and is stronger than the imported community attribution.

It does **not yet establish the identity of Mangulina recording `7a60dd1c-50a0-461b-896e-5a1c47d2fa7a`**. The official video lasts 5:30; the imported compilation recording is 292,706 ms (approximately 4:53), has no ISRC, and appears at position 8 of the 14-track La combinación perfecta edition. Video duration can include additional material, so the mismatch neither proves nor disproves identity. Original packaging or a direct recording/version match is still needed. No ownership, release placement or composer credit was changed on this evidence.

The official profile identifies itself as Aramis Camilo & La Nueva Organización Secreta and links to the artist's Instagram. An earlier official video about La varita's introduction concerns another recording and cannot settle these six compilation entries.
