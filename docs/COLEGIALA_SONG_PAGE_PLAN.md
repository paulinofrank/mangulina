# Colegiala song profile: evidence and presentation plan

## Verified catalog state

- **Work:** `Colegiala` (`646df393-978e-4457-a6c0-43e1b7a82ffd`), Spanish, composed in 1975.
- **Composer:** Walter León Aguilar. Qobuz and Shazam independently credit Walter León/Walter Leon.
- **Featured recording:** Alex Bueno (`698e33d6-115d-4801-8acf-73376269492f`, ISRC `USJ3V1497149`).
- **Lead performer:** Alex Bueno. Shazam specifies lead vocals.
- **Arranger:** Manuel Tejada. The database already held this credit; interviews and Dominican press also support his role in the recording and album.
- **Work coverage:** all six known Mangulina recordings are linked to the same Work.

## Research gaps kept out of the canonical credits

- Dominican press attributes the conga introduction to Canon Vilomar, but Mangulina has no reconciled identity for him and no source-level credit workflow for this claim yet.
- Ramón Orlando and Manuel Tejada are credited with album production/arrangement in reporting about the album. That does not establish a track-level producer credit for this recording.
- No reliable source reviewed identified a separate lyricist, backing vocalist roster, complete instrument lineup, or a label for the 1985 release represented on this page.

## Page improvement plan

1. **Make the Work the page identity.** Use one canonical `/songs/work-colegiala` page, with the title, composer, composition year, language, and a concise recording count at the top. Treat Alex Bueno's recording as the selected version rather than presenting it as the composition itself.
2. **Reduce the hero.** Use a two-column desktop hero and compact mobile hero: square cover at left, title and essential metadata at right. Keep only title, selected performer, year, duration, genre, and share action above the fold.
3. **Replace stacked cards with one credit panel.** Present `Composition` and `Selected recording` as two grouped columns. Show Walter León Aguilar under Composition; Alex Bueno and Manuel Tejada under the selected recording. Omit empty roles instead of showing absence messages.
4. **Add a version selector.** Put the six recordings in a compact chronological list or tabs. Each row should show performer, version label, year, duration, release, and identifiers. Selecting a row changes the recording credits, platforms, and releases without changing the Work identity.
5. **Move listening links beside the selected version.** Use a compact platform row immediately after the version metadata. Display only approved links and avoid a large nested platform card.
6. **Separate appearances from identity.** Place album and compilation appearances in a table or timeline below the credits, ordered by release year. The cover used in the hero should match the selected appearance.
7. **Remove unrelated profile content.** Do not place the full artist biography or a “More from Alex Bueno” carousel inside the song profile. Link the performer name and portrait to the artist page instead.
8. **Expose provenance quietly.** Add a small “Sources” disclosure beside each credit group. Show source name, observation date, and the exact supported assertion without exposing internal editorial notes.
9. **Use progressive disclosure on mobile.** Keep the Work summary, selected version, and primary credits open; collapse alternate versions, appearances, identifiers, and source details.
10. **Add missing-credit workflow later.** When editorial tooling supports per-credit evidence for recording musicians, add Canon Vilomar only after identity reconciliation, and add further vocals/instruments/producers only from exact recording-level sources.
