# La Gran Manzana / Víctor Roque editorial update

Date: 2026-11-15
Scope: Existing published group profile La Gran Manzana (UUID 908c3016-2aab-424e-9913-664e5f9a04ac) and founder Víctor Roque (UUID dc72e17e-660c-41bb-95e7-f5cf9c217c8e). Changes were made in Mangulina admin UI under the user's prior authorization to rename the orchestra and update the related profile and biographies. No discography, slug, deployments, or unrelated artist records were changed.

## Changes verified in admin

- Group display name and sort name: `Víctor Roque y La Gran Manzana` → `La Gran Manzana`.
- Existing group alias retained: `Víctor Roque y La Gran Manzana` for discovery; slug remains `victor-roque-y-la-gran-manzana`.
- Group primary role: `Singer` → `Orchestra`; removed `bandleader` from the group’s other roles because it describes Roque personally. Primary genre remains Merengue; musical genres remains empty, so it does not duplicate the primary genre.
- Founder relationships: Víctor Roque (from 1982, ongoing) and Henry Hierro (1982–1986) were visible in the relationship table after reselecting the group.
- Personal birth location stays Santo Domingo / Distrito Nacional. Biographies now state that Roque was born in Santo Domingo and that his maternal family is from San Cristóbal, which is the province with which he identifies.
- Group biographies were rewritten as group histories, with artist-reference links for all mentions of Víctor Roque, Henry Hierro and Fernando Villalona.
- English group biography: published revision 4. Spanish group biography: published revision 2.
- Both biographies describe the founding, first productions (1983–1985), Henry Hierro's departure in 1986, the 1995 Billboard Tropical Airplay success of «Ven Junto a Mí», the founders’ 1996 reunion, the 2022 anniversary-era digital catalog release, and the 2023 «Vamo’ Amanecer» release with Fernando Villalona.
