## Evidence and editorial choices

The supplied autobiographical account is near-verbatim to [Darío Sanjur's 2022 biography](https://upinforma.com/nuevo/info.php?cat=opinion&id=1082), so they were treated as one source chain. [El Día's 2012 interview](https://eldia.com.do/yanill-esta-de-regreso-con-balada-y-bachata/) confirms the personal name and 2012 return; [elCaribe's 2012 interview](https://www.elcaribe.com.do/sin-categoria/janyll-presenta-nuevo-album-discografico/) confirms the first album and *Señora*. [Manuel Tejada's discography listing](https://musicbase.net/manuel-tejada), a [photographed LP](https://www.ebay.com/itm/147600713113), and [Amazon Music](https://music.amazon.in/albums/B004U1CG7U) support *Pantera Agresiva* (1989) and *15 Grandes Éxitos* (1991). [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/veinte-a%C3%B1os-single/1540033609) dates «Veinte años» to 2020.

The exact original release year of *Máquina de placer* remains unresolved (1985 in the attached account, 1986 in another listing), so no year was added. The account duplicates a “second ACE award” under 1991 and 1995; both exact year and count were excluded. No birth year or unambiguous birth municipality was established, so date and city fields remain blank. No unverified chart rank, crowd size or live view counter was added. The artist's personal religious upbringing did not change her secular repertoire classification.
