## Transcript handling

The 23 files were searched for birth, training, group formation, awards, label dispute, recordings and later performances. Several are partial or noisy automatic transcripts; the 2026 «10 Preguntas Mix» file repeats substantial portions of the 2021 «10 Preguntas» interview and is not independent corroboration. An upload dated 2021 from «Ser Humano TV» describes 2006–07 events, so its filename is not treated as interview date. Comments on other artists' talent, private health and family life, and unsourced album-credit or release-date details are excluded. Supplied interview files were read only.
