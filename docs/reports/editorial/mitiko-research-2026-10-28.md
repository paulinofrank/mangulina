## Biography chronology and independent checks

- [Centro Cultural de España](https://ccesd.aecid.es/w/seis-d%C3%A9cadas-de-amargue-origen-y-evoluci%C3%B3n-de-la-bachata-1) documents an initial film career, La Gran Mawon, Los Sufridos' foundation in 2023, and sociocultural research.
- [La Galería's band interview](https://lagaleriamag.com/who-is-la-gran-mawon-in-conversation-with-the-up-coming-afrodominican-band/) describes the 2011 Colonial Zone jams and identifies Mawon Mitiko as guitarist and composer. [Retajila](https://retajila.com/descargas/UNA_ISLA_ES_UN_UNIVERSO_2016_2017.pdf) identifies him among the original continuing members, highlights composition and lyrics, dates the first single «Fuego Petró» to 2013 and the seven-track debut EP to 2016.
- [DISCOLAI, 2019](https://discolai.com/2019/03/08/melancolia-tropical-y-el-subelo-mawon-que-resuena-en-el-timpano/) and [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/co/album/melancolia-tropical-single/1454848328) establish «Melancolia Tropical» as a **single**, correcting the supplied account's implied album classification. [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/radio-katharsis/1704765111) lists the 2023 album «Radio Katharsis».
- [Diario Libre](https://www.diariolibre.com/revista/musica/2024/08/28/bachata-urbana-con-anthony-santos/2833017) identifies José Julio Bello among the writers of Los Sufridos' «Lleno Contigo». [DCOCOTE](https://dcocote.com/2024/06/20/los-sufridos-chivi/) and [DCOCOTE](https://dcocote.com/2024/09/16/los-sufridos-bad-gyal-duro-de-verdad-pt-2/) list Mítiko among, not as sole, producers of two 2024 releases. The supplied «Postalita» individual credit was not independently confirmed and is omitted.
- [Casa de América's film program](https://www.casamerica.es/sites/default/files/1.%20HOJAS%20DE%20SALA/MALDECANA_HOJADESALA.pdf) gives original-music credit on «Mal de caña» to José Julio Bello “Mítiko” and Marcos Arias. [Audiomack](https://audiomack.com/billete-mc/song/por-ahi) credits José Julio Bello Castillo as producer of Billete MC's 2023 «Por Ahí»; that recording's AFRK Music label aligns with [the ONAPI registration](https://www.onapi.gov.do/transparencia/index.php/institucional/publicaciones/3516-boletines-de-registros-publicados-del-2020-al-2026/2160-publicaciones-2020-2022/2090-publicaciones-2022/2544-diciembre-pub-2022?download=12883%3Apublicacion-15-de-diciembre-2022), but no music/credit row is being created here.
- [Rutgers University](https://africanastudies.rutgers.edu/insurgent-intersections/themes/global-lived-experiences-of-anti-blackness-2023-24/2023-24-events/386-workshop-afro-dominican-music-percussion-workshop-with-la-gran-mawon) lists a February 2024 Afro-Dominican music workshop with Mitiko Mawon and band members.
