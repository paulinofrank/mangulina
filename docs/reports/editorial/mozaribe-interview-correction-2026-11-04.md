## What the source says

- In the 2026-06-28 *10 Preguntas* interview transcript, at about 50:30–54:04, Frantoni Santana describes an attempted new Mozaribe lineup after the initial band separated. He says two Cuban singers, Judy and a younger niece, were contracted and rehearsing for a Santiago presentation planned for 28 January 1994. Shortly before the show they told him they had accepted work with Fernando Villalona. Santana explicitly addresses Villalona without blaming him personally. He also recounts the investor withdrawing after this setback.
- The 2026-06-22 transcript is the same interview, not independent corroboration. A separate 2026-09-18 *Trayectoria RD* interview revisits the story and says Villalona may not have known the circumstances. Thus the public biography must attribute the account to Santana. It must not state a deliberate poaching, conspiracy or causation as established fact.
- The separate 2026-09-18 interview attributes the orchestra's initial concept to a conversation between Santana and violinist Carlos Jáquez on calle El Conde; Santana proposed joining violins and merengue brass. Its automatic transcription is rough. The 2026-10-27 [Jazz en Dominicana interview with Bienvenido “Micky” Creales](https://www.jazzendominicana.com/2026/10/jazz-en-dominicana-serie-entrevistas.html?m=0) independently lists Mozaribe among the groups he played with. The [Space Coast Symphony's Carlos Jáquez biography](https://spacecoastsymphony.org/our-family/our-violins/) identifies him as Mozaribe's director and producer.
