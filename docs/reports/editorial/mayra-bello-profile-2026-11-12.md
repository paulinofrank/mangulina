## Research distinctions

Her Facebook profile says she is from Santo Domingo and lives in Teaneck, New Jersey. In-profile searches for “cumpleaños”, “Happy Birthday” and “cumple” found a greeting dated 20 November 2024 and Mayra's 27 November 2023 post saying her birthday was one week earlier. Her own birthday thanks dated 22 November 2022 and 2025 could be late posts. The evidence points to 20 November, but no birth year is established; `date_of_birth` stays empty and the public biography omits an exact birthday.

An [El Día interview](https://eldia.com.do/mayra-bello-abraza-su-propio-proyecto-musical-basado-en-merengue/) recounts her early hotel performances, La Artillería, the proposal that led to Los Toros Band, and her work with Milly Quezada. A later [first-person interview](https://impactolatino.com/mayra-bello-puedo-interpretar-cualquier-genero-musical/) describes her musical training, solo recordings and the 2025 Christmas album. [Primera Hora](https://www.primerahora.com/entretenimiento/musica/notas/milly-quezada-y-mayra-bello-se-pelean-por-un-mismo-hombre/) documents the duet «La Infiel» and its writer Kike Traver. The [Apple Music catalog](https://music.apple.com/us/artist/mayra-bello/1196940275) independently documents «Con Más Dulzura», «Déjame Vivir» and «Mayimbeando en Navidad, Vol. 3».

The supplied bio sheet identifies her separately from Irisneyda Santos of The New York Band; no New York Band affiliation is carried into this profile. Its claimed sleeve credits for Los Toros Band's debut have not been independently inspected here, so the public biography uses her own account of participating in the orchestra's founding period, without assigning an unsupported track credit. The bolero tribute is described by Mayra's official channel but lacks a securely verified release year.
