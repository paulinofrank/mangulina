## Independent research and source assessment

- [Joseph Cáceres / Merengala, 2021](https://merengala.blogspot.com/2021/09/ramoncito-diaz.html) reproduces a profile credited to Jesús Torres Tejeda's *Fichero Artístico Dominicano*, via Ismael Hernández. This is one evidence lineage, not three independent accounts.
- [Diario Libre, 30 Jan 2007](https://www.diariolibre.com/amp/revista/fallece-el-compositor-y-publicista-ramn-daz-DLDL124465) independently confirms name, death, major songs, agency work and Don Chencho. Its stated age of 66 conflicts with 1935-08-31.
- [Alexis Méndez, *Salsa desde mi balcón*, footnote 25](https://ign.gob.do/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/SALSA-DESDE-MI-BALCON-final-digital.pdf) gives 31 Aug 1935 and attributes «Un pie aquí y el otro allá» to Ramoncito.
- [Máximo Jiménez, Revista Global](https://revistaglobal.org/los-anos-dorados-del-merengue-y-el-arte-de-sus-compositores/amp/) distinguishes the two Ramón Díaz identities and documents the merengue repertoire and release years.
- [José Rivera Damirón interview account](https://www.10noticias.com.do/politica/don-chencho-el-historico-comercial-de-balaguer-se-ideo-en-la-carcel-la-victoria/) says Damirón proposed the campaign concept and Ramoncito created it; biography must not claim exclusive conception.
- [Former Lotería Nacional official's account](https://pronosticamedia.com/mis-vivencias-en-la-loteria-nacional/) independently identifies Ramoncito's Publicitaria Trío and «Manos limpias» campaign.

Severe criminal allegations in anonymous blog comments are not treated as verified biographical facts. A quoted opinion column does not identify him by name in the text copied there. Do not place these claims in a public biography without independent, attributable legal evidence.
