## Findings and exact scope

- Nicky Soul: the two versions carry the same dates, career sequence and uncertainties. Joan Minaya is mentioned without an artist reference in both. Convert those two mentions to canonical links, preserving all prose and other links.
- Felipe Jorge: the Spanish version has an unattributed claim of decades of silence and 2025–26 interviews not corroborated in the current source set. It also embeds the noncanonical `Nicki Soul` in a quotation. The English version lacks the other ensembles and recordings documented in Felipe's 2005 interview. Replace both with independent, factually aligned narratives: early work with Félix del Rosario and Nicky Soul in «Grupo Félix» (formed 1984); selected repertoire; Felipe's later work with Los Kukos, Elías Santana, Zona Roja and Henry Hierro; his announced own orchestra and first album in 2005 (do not claim release); invitation to the 2007 Félix tribute; historical concert audio distributed in 2025 (do not imply a 2025 performance). Link existing artist rows for Félix, Nicky, Toño Rosario and Henry Hierro. Keep birthplace and DOB unstated.
- Roland Roll: both versions have the same basic chronology but contain subjective praise, duplicate the album/arrangers in the Legacy paragraph, and display two noncanonical link labels (`Juan Valdez` instead of `Juan Valdez Ybet`, `Luis Díaz` instead of `Luis "Terror" Días`). Rewrite both concisely with the verified 1989 album, 1990 editions, early 1990s second production, later U.S. activity, canonical linked arrangers, and a nonrepetitive closing. Avoid stating current residence beyond what dated sources support. No claim that Felipe Jorge sang on Roland's album: one collector blog asserts this, but the inspected discographic report does not confirm it from the sleeve.
