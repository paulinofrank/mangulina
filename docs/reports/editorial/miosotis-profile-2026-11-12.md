## Research additions and caution

- [El Día's 2023 interview](https://eldia.com.do/miosotis-se-abre-paso-en-la-musica-a-ritmo-de-salsa/) names her father Víctor Malagón, describes her audition with Wilfrido Vargas, Altamira Banda Show at age 15 and «Dame Vida» with Cherito. It also documents her salsa career and «Y Qué» promotion.
- Her [official discography](https://www.miosotismusic.com/discography) dates «Hazme Soñar» 1995, «Nace Una Estrella» 1997 and «Ardiente» 2000. [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/ardiente/165339660) independently dates «Ardiente» 17 October 2000. Her official biography has a conflicting 2003 date for that album; use 2000.
- [Qobuz credits](https://www.qobuz.com/us-en/album/a-mi-manera-sergio-vargas/ji443btufjaua) identify Miosotis as a main artist with Sergio Vargas on «Oportunidad Perdida». The [Latin Recording Academy](https://www.latingrammy.com/en/awards/categories/best-merengue-bachata-album/2023/) records «A Mi Manera» and Romeo Santos's «Fórmula, Vol. 3» as co-winners in 2023. This is an award for the album, not a separate individual solo award for Miosotis.
- [2024 EP announcement](https://musicalatina.com/miosotis-lanza-nuevo-ep-como-te-va-mi-amor-salsa-con-el-sello-jn/) documents five-track «Dame Salsa» and the current `@miosotismusic` accounts. [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/me-cans%C3%A9-de-ti/6778009068?i=6778009069) and [2026 coverage](https://diariodetodos.do/farandula-y-mas/miosotis-presenta-me-canse-de-ti-una-declaracion-de-fuerza-desamor-y-empoderamiento/) document «Me Cansé de Ti» and her writing credit. No discography rows will be created in this task.
- The supplied sheet's claim that both parents were Dominican is narrower on her [own biography](https://www.miosotismusic.com/biography), which describes Dominican and Puerto Rican ancestry. The bios therefore avoid claiming both parents were Dominican. The sheet's first-person editorial commentary and unverified sales figures were not adopted.
