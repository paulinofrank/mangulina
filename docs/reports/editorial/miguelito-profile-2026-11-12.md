## Research corrections and context

The supplied sheet dates «Poder Musical» to 1978. The US copyright registration reproduced at [Copyright Encyclopedia](https://www.copyrightencyclopedia.com/belkis-concepcion-and-las-chicas-del-can-con-wilfrido/), [Qobuz's Karen metadata](https://www.qobuz.com/us-en/album/poder-musical-wilfrido-vargas/h3sd9twi1ni7b), and a contemporary [March 1979 Record World review](https://www.worldradiohistory.com/Archive-All-Music/Record-World/70s/79/RW-1979-03-10.pdf) document the 1979 album. The biography separates his 1978 recruitment from that album's publication. Qobuz credits «Wilfrido Dame un Consejo» to Ramón Díaz and Wilfrido Vargas; the [AGN catalog entry](https://colecciones.agn.gob.do/opac/ficha.php?codopac=OP003&idpag=967010062&informatico=00276538SR) credits Ramón Díaz for a later LP appearance. Do not assert sole authorship by Ramoncito Díaz.

A 2008 [Primera Hora interview](https://www.primerahora.com/entretenimiento/musica/notas/hace-sacros-sus-exitos-merengueros/) identifies «Soy un hombre bendecido» as his first Christian album in 2001 and «Gozo del cielo» as his second album in 2008. [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/artist/mi-amigo-miguelito/280971296) and the directly opened Spotify catalog list a 2008 digital edition of the first album, plus «Todo Esta Bien» (2015), «Tengo Nueva Vida» (2022) and later singles. The biography distinguishes the first release account from digital dating. The 2008 interview confirms collaborations with Jorge Gómez, Sandy Reyes and Jossie Esteban and describes the reworked songs «A Dios toda la gloria» and «A mi esposa». No discography rows are imported in this task.
