Michan Leonardo is a bachata singer associated with the Dominican recording scene of the mid-2000s. His documented work includes «Adiós Good Bye» and the radio promotion of «Llámame» and «No Puedo Vivir Sin Ti». His performances of «Llámame» and «Sin ti la vida se me va» remain available on digital compilations.

Radio promotion in 2006

On November 13, 2006, Diario Libre reported plans to promote «No Puedo Vivir Sin Ti», written by Robert Félix, after «Llámame». His representative, Rafael Alcántara, described the change of single as a continuation of the singer’s radio campaign.

The album «Adiós Good Bye»

The report documented a ten-song bachata album for «Necho Records», recorded at «Estudios Emcas» in Santo Domingo and arranged by Mártires de León. According to Alcántara, the repertoire included compositions by Frantoni Santana, Miguel Braho, Félix Veloz and Michan himself. It also named «Otro Ocupa Mi Lugar» and «Sin ti la vida se me va» among the promoted tracks.

Compilation appearances

Spotify and Apple Music list «Llámame» and «Sin Ti la Vida Se Me Va» on «Bachatas Pal' Pueblo», with an edition date of 2007. Spotify also shows appearances on «Bachata Pal Pueblo» and «Bachata Pal Pueblo Vol 2», dated 2015. These are compilation appearances, and their platform dates do not establish the original recording dates.

Legacy

The surviving record places Michan Leonardo among the singers seeking an audience in 2000s bachata. Contemporary reporting and digital editions preserve part of his work, while leaving his personal history and later performing activity unresolved.
