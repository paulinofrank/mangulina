# Sony Ovalles — index of songs in the supplied audio

## Scope and confidence

All 12 supplied files were processed, totalling approximately **2 h 53 min 06 s**. The host closes the Sony Ovalles tribute in **Part 08 around 08:25**. **Part 09 around 02:40** introduces a separate bolero special. The later boleros are not assigned to Ovalles merely because they appear in this recording.

This index is based on automatic Spanish transcription, targeted reprocessing of musical excerpts, the original video's music metadata, and external title/credit references. It has **not** been verified by listening directly to every passage. Times are relative to the individual MP3 and approximate; they identify evidence passages, not necessarily exact track starts. Some brief or instrumental selections remain unidentified. Do not treat this as a certified complete playlist.

**Attribution** below means what the broadcast claims. It does not independently prove a historical credit. **Identification** concerns which song is playing; these are different questions.
