# Sony Ovalles — evidence notes

This research separates the supplied radio recording, machine transcription, and external discographic evidence. It is not a complete lifetime catalogue. No database changes were made.

## Initial audio findings

- Part 01, 11:22–14:25: host introduces a programme about Sony Ovalles's arrangements and contributions to merengue and salsa. Machine recognition varies the spelling of his name. Arrangement is not equivalent to authorship.
- Part 01, 14:42 through Part 02 opening: first selection, La Yola (identified by lyrics and original video's music metadata), Wilfrido Vargas. Programme-level arrangement attribution; exact recording/version remains unverified.
- Part 02, approximately 07:02–07:35: host explicitly announces Volveré a mi tierra, Sergio Vargas, with musical arrangements by Sony Ovalles.
- Part 02, approximately 13:15: host introduces another song he arranged for Sergio Vargas. Title awaits subsequent evidence.

## External corroboration and limitations

- Máximo Jiménez, Revista Global issue 104, printed p.45 / PDF p.47, names La yola as a Jaime Shanlate song coauthored with Sonny Ovalle, recorded by Wilfrido Vargas in 1983. https://revistaglobal.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/G104-v19.pdf
- Collector's description of a 1987 Sergio Vargas / Los Hijos del Rey single credits Volveré a mi tierra to José María Rodríguez, arrangement Sonny Ovalles. It distinguishes a Manuel Tejada arrangement of Noche on the single from a Sonny Ovalles LP version. Collector evidence, not an inspected label scan: https://clasicospagoza.blogspot.com/2010/
- Commentary on arrangers associates Buscando tus besos, Ave de paso, Cuando estés con él (Rubby Pérez); Si tú eres mi hombre y yo tu mujer, Sueño con besar tu boca, Cole (The New York Band); and Ta pillao, Estúpido (Las Chicán) with Sonny Ovalles. Secondary commentary, recording versions not verified: https://www.noticiasvillariva.com.do/talento-y-mercadeo
- Original video music metadata identifies eight visible titles, despite heading stating ten: La Yola; Volveré a mi tierra; Buscando tus besos; Ave de paso; Cumandé; Mi tonto amor; Tan Pillao; Cole. Metadata alone does not establish arrangement or composition. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_03X1_pmdws
- A viewer comment on that video disputes the arrangement of La tierra tembló, attributing it to Juan Valdez. Neither the comment nor a general tribute should settle a disputed recording credit without stronger documentation.
