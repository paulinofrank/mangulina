## Disputed or edition-dependent credits

- **La tierra tembló — Sergio Vargas:** the career profile associates it with Ovalles; a viewer of the original video attributes the arrangement to Juan Valdez. The viewer comment is not definitive. Keep unresolved pending original recording documentation. [Video and comments](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_03X1_pmdws)
- **Noche — Sergio Vargas:** a collector describes a Manuel Tejada arrangement on a single and a Sonny Ovalles arrangement on the LP. Do not apply one credit indiscriminately. [Collector's description](https://clasicospagoza.blogspot.com/2010/)
- **Fiesta para dos:** album production reported for Ovalles does not establish a track arrangement credit. The title song's reproduced registration lists Víctor Víctor as writer and Manuel Tejada as arranger. [Registration listing](https://www.copyrightencyclopedia.com/ese-caso-lo-tenemos-a-baila-to-el-mundo-no-llores-por-ella/)

## Album-level production

Ovalles is reported as producer of Rubby Pérez's *Buscando tus besos* and *Fiesta para dos*, and of early Las Chicas del Can albums. These are album-level statements, not proof he wrote or arranged every track. [Inter News Service](https://internewsservice.com/2020/12/13/r-dominicana-fallece-de-un-infarto-cardiaco-el-reconocido-pianista-y-productor-musical-sony-ovalles/)
